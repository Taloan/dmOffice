## Programs in the Docker image

The programs are installed from the package sources of the base image (Ubuntu 24.04) and called as separate processes; dmOffice does not link against them. The license texts of the packages, including their dependencies, are in the image under `/usr/share/doc/<package>/copyright`. The Photo Engine is a separate Python process: its Python packages come from PyPI in fixed versions, with their license files in the image under `/opt/photo-engine/venv/lib/python3.12/site-packages/*.dist-info/`; the models and their licenses are under `/opt/photo-engine/models/`.
